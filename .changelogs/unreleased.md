## New

- Looking up something from a card now shows a Tank tab to fill or empty cells for free, and a Generators tab with the generators and boilers that burn or make it.
- Screenshots can be saved as a GIF with the wires flowing.

## Better

- Select some cards and the overview shows just what goes in and out of them. You can turn it off in settings.
- My plans sorts by tier, power and machine count, and filters by tier, power and what a plan makes or takes.

## Fixed

- The planner no longer freezes the game for seconds the first time it opens (and on NEI recipe pages).
- Plans opened from a plan link, code or file keep their icon, and boxes keep their background theme.
